\section{Thermal Operating Environments}

\subsection{Automotive IVI Thermal Context}

The reference system is an IVI head unit operating across cabin temperature, solar loading, enclosure heat soak, and vehicle-speed regimes. The thermal controller must handle both slow heat accumulation and abrupt workload changes while avoiding unnecessary fan noise in quiet conditions.

\subsection{Operating Regimes}

\midrule

Urban driving & 10--50 km/h & moderate acoustic masking; allow gradual cooling response \\
