\section{Thermal Zone Mapping}\label{sec:zone-mapping}

\subsection{Zone Model}

A zone is a logical thermal region such as \texttt{soc}, \texttt{amp}, \texttt{tuner}, or \texttt{board}. A zone may have one or more sensors, a temperature aggregation policy, trip points, a governor, and one or more actuators it can request.

\subsection{Reference Zone Configuration}

The shipped reference configuration (\texttt{esp32-c3-standalone.json}, reused with platform-specific I/O paths on the Linux Pi\,4 and HIL bench builds) defines a single \texttt{soc\_zone}:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item one sensor, \texttt{soc} --- a DS18B20 probe --- with a Q16.16 IIR filter and a 5\,s staleness limit;
    \item one actuator, \texttt{main\_fan}, driven by a step-wise governor with \texttt{max} sensor aggregation;
    \item four trip points at 30, 45, 60, and 85\,$^\circ$C, each with a 2\,$^\circ$C hysteresis band, mapping to cooling states 1--4;
    \item a per-actuator \texttt{state\_pwm} staircase of $[0, 100, 160, 220, 255]$, so the fan idles off below 30\,$^\circ$C and reaches full duty once the 85\,$^\circ$C trip is crossed;
    \item a zone fallback of 85\,$^\circ$C, substituted when the sensor goes stale so a lost probe fails toward cooling rather than away from it.
\end{itemize}

The hysteresis band is uniform at 2\,$^\circ$C: a trip de-asserts only after the temperature drops 2\,$^\circ$C below its threshold, which prevents fan-state chatter when a reading dwells on a boundary. Adding a second fan or probe is a configuration-only change --- the firmware loops over the declared sensor and actuator counts rather than hardcoding slot~0.
